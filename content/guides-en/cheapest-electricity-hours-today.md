# Cheapest hours for electricity today: how to plan your usage

On a dynamic contract, the price you pay for electricity can change every hour (or every quarter-hour) [1][2]. Shift your usage to the cheap moments and you pay less for the same kWh. How much you save depends on your usage and that day's prices.

## 1. Check today's prices

[Electricity price today](https://cerberostech.github.io/stroomprijs/en/electricity-price-today/) shows the market price per hour. The [homepage](https://cerberostech.github.io/stroomprijs/en/) shows the cheapest continuous 3-hour block. These are bare market prices excluding VAT; your supplier adds its markup, energy tax and VAT on top.

## 2. Look ahead to tomorrow

According to the ACM, tomorrow's prices are available every day at around 16:00 [2]. You'll find them on [Electricity price tomorrow](https://cerberostech.github.io/stroomprijs/en/electricity-price-tomorrow/), so you can plan the evening before.

## 3. Shift what you can

Appliances that use a lot of power and can run at a flexible time are the best candidates:

- **Charging an electric car**: set a start time in the car or on the charger.
- **Washing machine, tumble dryer and dishwasher**: use the delayed start.
- **Water heater or heat pump**: some systems can respond to prices; check with your installer.

The fridge, lighting and cooking stay as they are, and that's fine.

## 4. Add it to your calendar

This site publishes a calendar file with the cheapest hours for each day: [goedkoopste-uren.ics](https://cerberostech.github.io/stroomprijs/goedkoopste-uren.ics). Subscribe to it in your calendar app.

## Keep in mind

- If your supplier bills per quarter-hour, prices can differ within an hour; since 1 October 2025 the market has set 96 quarter-hour prices per day [2][3].
- A cheap hour on the exchange doesn't always mean a low final price: the fixed markups and taxes apply every hour.

For more background, read [Dynamic electricity prices in the Netherlands: how do they work?](dynamic-electricity-price-how-it-works).
