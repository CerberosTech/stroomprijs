## Who makes this site?

Stroomprijs is a small, independent project by CerberosTech. The site is not affiliated with any energy supplier, EPEX SPOT or the Dutch government. There is no editorial team; the prices are fetched automatically and the site is rebuilt every hour.

## Sources

- **Prices:** EnergyZero, which publishes the EPEX SPOT day-ahead prices for the Dutch bidding zone. The same auction results are on the ENTSO-E Transparency Platform.
- **Taxes:** the energy tax rates of the Belastingdienst (the Dutch Tax and Customs Administration) and the VAT rate.
- **Contract information:** the ACM (Netherlands Authority for Consumers and Markets) and its consumer site ACM ConsuWijzer.

## How the prices are calculated

- The market prices shown are **bare day-ahead prices, excl. VAT**.
- The "all-in" estimate is calculated per hour as (market price + supplier markup + energy tax) × 1.21. The markup is 0 by default; enter the markup from your own contract for a better estimate. Fixed costs are not included. See "How we calculate".
- Every page shows when the prices were last fetched. If a fetch fails, the page says so and shows the last known prices.
- Guides show an "Updated on" date for the last change to their content.

## Affiliate links

Some pages contain affiliate links, for example to bol.com (a Dutch online shop). If you buy something through such a link, we may receive a small commission. You pay nothing extra, and it has no influence on the prices or the explanations.
